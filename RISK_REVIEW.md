# Portfolio construction review

## Repository audit and implementation decision

Review base `d17a0f0`. The website is a decision aid for a public USD paper model. The monitor consumes a pinned upstream commit and records consumed blob hashes. This repository has no mandate-wide book, client asset register, broker account or fill feed.

| Decision input | Source of truth and qualification |
|---|---|
| Book and valuation | Upstream `live_track.json` supplies effective model weights at the portfolio anchor and the separate forward marked NAV extension. The normalised rows represent allocations to instruments. They are unsuitable as actual shares, intraday drifted weights or executed trades. |
| Allocation | The deployed upstream construction combines US sector breadth, cross-asset trend, thematic momentum and European sector breadth in a 35/35/10/20 base mix. The EM tilt draws from cross-asset trend. The breadth overlay can move capital into short Treasuries. A defensive Treasury holding can also arise inside a sleeve. |
| History | `risk_overlay.json` supplies deployed and gate-only curves. `multi_strategy.json` supplies ungated and sleeve curves. The registry and upstream execution contract specify Monday close, with holiday roll-forward. Historic weekly reconstructions and legacy Friday labels require verification against effective fills before episode research. Reconstructed targets, restated backtests and prospectively captured history require distinct labels. |
| Costs | Model NAV embeds the engine’s assumed 5 bp switch cost. The turnover sensitivity is illustrative. Exchange rolls, bid–ask spreads, option premiums and broker collateral are outside this convention. |
| Existing analytics | `adapter.py`, `metrics.py`, historical attribution, benchmark/FX and calendar guards already provide drawdown, realised volatility, capture, sleeve correlations, action history, return and risk attribution. The covariance display uses covered long positions, renormalised weights and some local prices. Using it for USD whole-book forecasts would conceal omitted risk. |
| Completeness | The base snapshot covers 17 public model positions. The repository has neither a complete set of discretionary sleeves nor historical constituent weights, verified duration, executable derivatives prices, option surfaces, liquidity or margin schedules. A trade proxy or listing currency alone cannot establish economic exposure. |

## Priorities and research feasibility

1. **Phase 1, shadow diagnostics.** Add one optional dataset field, a versioned scenario and budget policy, tests and a compact card in Risk & Regime. Use signed model capital weights, explicit instrument coverage and exclusive scenario buckets. Show the source dates, shortfalls and the ranking universe beside results. Preserve old clients and old datasets. No feed, upstream calculation, rebalance, alert or order dependency.
2. **Phase 2, subject to review.** Introduce a private position and instrument master before aggregating other sleeves. Source constituent snapshots, economic currency, contract multipliers, dated duration and calibrated USD factor histories. Prefer dated direct look-through, then out-of-sample regressions, then documented proxies. Select one method per exposure. Estimate marginal portfolio risk with common-date USD returns and reconcile it to standalone risk. Add reverse stress by horizon, pre-trade simulation, VaR model comparison and exceedance testing once inputs and constraints are agreed.
3. **Phase 3, separate research decision.** Freeze the same prior-close defensive signal and set risk and protection matching criteria before examining outcomes. Reuse the existing costed defensive futures and re-entry studies. Their rejected implementations do not settle the option or risk-matched question. Compare cash with an observed collateral series, futures with contract-level settlements and net carry, and options only with timestamped strikes, expiries and two-sided quotes. Report hypothetical repricing separately. Predefine a positive-return event threshold, lag positions, cluster overlapping episodes, and measure 1/5/10/20-session outcomes, re-entry and upside forgone against a like-for-like unhedged book. The model starts after 2009. Later episodes require verified effective weights and contemporaneous decisions before attribution.

The present data support deterministic price shocks on a model snapshot and historical tails of the path actually reported. They cannot establish whole-fund resilience, margin headroom, causal rebound opportunity cost or the superiority of another hedge implementation. No new hedge backtest is part of this change.

## Phase 1 methodology

The normal pipeline attaches `construction_risk` when the public paper portfolio has a matching policy under `portfolios/risk/`. Version 2 contains seven two-sided shocks, one global equity decline and seven joint scenarios. Each experiment holds the model anchor weights fixed. It assumes an instantaneous move, with no intervening fills, financing, trading costs or margin calls. The historical NAV metrics have their own end date. The dashboard health clock indicates when current freshness requires rechecking.

**Scenario P&L.** Each allowlisted position belongs to one price bucket. A joint scenario supplies one all-in USD price change for each bucket. For an isolated experiment, omitted covered buckets are expressly held flat. A named unpriced bucket or an unrecognised instrument prevents a whole-book total. The covered subtotal retains its original NAV denominator. A zero result under the dedicated US technology proxy measures only that bucket. Embedded semiconductor or issuer exposures through other funds require constituent look-through. The commodity experiment shocks a broad basket. The yield experiment requires dated duration before a bond price impact can be estimated. The joint bond moves are explicit fund price assumptions. No instrument duration, option sensitivity or yield-to-price multiplier has been inferred.

**Attribution and exposure.** Multiply each signed weight by its bucket price change. Sum over positions, factors or dated sleeve legs to reconcile to the covered subtotal. Sleeve legs use the engine's anchor-date books, tilt funding and gate fraction. Round-off is allocated proportionally only within the source precision tolerance. An unavailable sleeve leaves the other dated books intact. Any unmatched balance is shown under unresolved ownership. Conflicting legs in excess of the position invalidate its split. The Treasury reserve can belong to several sleeves. Net and gross exposure sum signed and absolute weights of the covered fully funded instruments, respectively. Net before hedges removes only designated hedge positions. Explicit hedge P&L is a subset of the position total. The reduction in risk from a gate requires a separate ungated counterfactual. Factor concentration here measures capital assigned to a bucket. Portfolio beta, delta-equivalent notional and margin require different inputs.

**Gap ceilings.** The initial 0.50% of NAV per-name budget and bucket shocks are illustrative. A 10% adverse move with that budget gives a 5% weight ceiling. For a short, the adverse move is upwards. The loss column is a positive magnitude. The tightest applicable budget across portfolio default, asset class, instrument type and owning sleeve controls. A sleeve-specific budget applies to its share of a shared holding. Current sleeve allocations, optional name caps and gross factor headroom can tighten the ceiling further. Other positions remain fixed in the headroom calculation. The output is a ceiling under tested constraints. A final trading size also needs verified volatility sizing and liquidity. Unsupported nonlinear and leveraged instruments return unavailable. A stop order supplies no guaranteed overnight execution price.

**Historical tails.** Use the USD deployed model curve and its separately supplied post-anchor extension. Reindex closes to NYSE sessions before differencing, omit returns touching a missing close, and require 252 valid returns including the last reported session. Annualised volatility uses sample standard deviation times √252. Sort one-day losses from worst to best. At confidence α, historical VaR selects rank ceil((1−α)×252). Expected shortfall at 97.5% averages the worst 6.3 observations with fractional weight on the seventh. A loss-positive convention allows negative estimates in an all-gain sample. Tail estimates describe a changing historical model. A current-holdings distribution, probability of a scenario and maximum potential loss are separate questions. The short tail sample makes a single VaR exceedance weak evidence.

**Coverage and shared exposures.** Each scenario reports absolute model weight divided by NAV in three categories: shocked, explicitly flat and unpriced. Their sum equals gross invested weight. A calculable total can have zero shocked weight. The recovery row identifies the gate state at the model anchor. For an open gate and a priced reserve, its separate counterfactual applies the configured fraction to the reserve and the remainder to the existing book under identical price assumptions. An active or unverified gate has no second switch applied.

Gap results retain any demonstrable breach if ownership is incomplete. The ceiling is withheld, and other incomplete assessments are indeterminate. The common trade proxy subtotal uses the signed net weight times one common symmetric gap magnitude. Opposing weights offset only within that specified proxy experiment. Divergent or missing shock magnitudes withhold the loss estimate. The subtotal is informational and leaves each instrument's flag intact.

**Governance.** Stress results are informational. Breaches of a shadow gap ceiling invite review. Hard limits are empty. Keep any official exception record private, with the limit, observation date, rationale, owner, status, approver and expiry. A policy version change requires review of mappings, assumptions and tests. No code path creates orders or writes back to source weights.

**Reproduction.** Run `python -m pytest tests/ -q` and `python scripts/verify_shadow_replay.py <upstream-git-clone>`. The replay checks all nine source blobs from the audited published vintage, runs both pipeline versions with vendor downloads disabled and compares all sixteen pre-existing data sections after excluding acquisition and build timestamps. It writes a local preview under `.preview/risk-replay/web/` with the published benchmark curves restored. The production workflow continues to own generated Pages files.
